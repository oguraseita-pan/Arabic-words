Data & learning notes

The 100-word dataset, vowel-marked Arabic, romanization, examples, verb forms, noun plurals and gender, and adjective forms come from the previous Arabic 100 version.

Fusha (Modern Standard Arabic) is used here. Spoken Arabic can differ by region; final case endings are often not pronounced in everyday speech.
